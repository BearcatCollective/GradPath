# **GradPath** 

**Team Members:** Abhishek Rautela, Oriana Hernandez, Michael McFarland, Ian Collamer 

## **Economic** 

Economic constraints factor into GradPath because our goal is to complete the project with a budget of $0. Because of this, we plan to use free or open-source tools and the Microsoft Azure free tier for hosting. This creates a trade-off because keeping the project free may limit its storage and processing power, making it harder to support many UC students at once. 

## **Professional** 

Professional constraints factor into GradPath because creating accurate graduation plans requires knowledge of UC’s degree requirements and advising process. We chose a UC academic advisor as our project advisor because they have experience helping students plan courses and meet graduation requirements. Their guidance will help us determine whether we are allowed to access UC’s internal information. If access is not permitted, we will rely on students uploading the degree audits manually and publicly available course information. 

## **Security** 

Security constraints factor into GradPath because students will submit degree audits containing private academic information. GradPath will not require user accounts and will only keep uploaded degree audits temporarily while creating a graduation plan. This reduces the risk of stored student information being exposed, but it also means that returning users may need to upload their degree audits again. 

## **Ethical** 

Ethical constraints factor into GradPath because UC students may rely on its recommendations when making decisions that affect their graduation plans. GradPath will follow UC’s official prerequisites and course availability as well as the advise of our advisor all the while, making it clear that the system is an advising aid and not a replacement for an academic advisor. It will provide multiple plans based on student preferences and require users to verify generated schedules with an academic advisor rather than presenting any plan as guaranteed to satisfy graduation requirements.
